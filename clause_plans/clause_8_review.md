8. Post offices Holidays  –  The following holidays are observed as Post Office 
holidays :- 
 
 (1)    Republic Day   (26th  January) 
 (2)    Independence Day  (15th  August) 
 (3)    Mahatma Gandhi’s Birthday (2nd October) 
(4)   Idul-Zuha (Bakr-Id)   


(5)   Muharram 
(6)   Idul-Fitr 
(7)   Good Friday   (Friday before Easter Sunday) 
(8)   Christmas Day   (25th  December) 
(9)   Budha Purnima 
(10)  Guru Nanak Birthday 
(11)  Mahavir Jayanti 
 
 In add ition the post offices observe post office holidays on 5 other days which 
vary from circle to circle. 
 
 
PAYMENT OF POSTAGE
