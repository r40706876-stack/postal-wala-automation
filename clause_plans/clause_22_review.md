## Poora clause (PDF se)
```
22. Desirability of posting early-  If articles are posted just at the time the mails are due to 
be dispatched there is heavy congestion in Post Offices and R.M.S. Offices and there is likelihood 
of detention and delay.  The public are, therefore, advised to post their mails as and when t hey are 
ready without waiting for the last clearance of mails from the Post Office and to endeavour to post 
as early in the day as possible.
```
