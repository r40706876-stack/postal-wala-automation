## Poora clause (PDF se)
```
20. Presentation of large official and other letters in bulk at the window of the van or 
Post Office -  Large official or other letters, which owing to their size, cannot be posted in the 
letter-boxes, may be received by hand at the window of the van or post office.  There is also no 
objection to receiving at the window of the van or office, letters or packets posted in bulk 
provided, the postage and the late fee, if any, are fully prepaid.
```
