## Poora clause (PDF se)
```
23. Manner of affixing postage stamps  - Stamps affixed to a letter or packet must be 
completely adherent to the article itself and not to any part of its contents or any separate label or 
other device tied or attached to the article.  The stamps should be affixed to the right hand top 
corner of the address side.
```
