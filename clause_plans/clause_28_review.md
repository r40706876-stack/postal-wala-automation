## Poora clause (PDF se)
```
28. Mails for Navy Personnel serving in INS Ships  –  Mail for Navy Personnel on board 
I.N.S. ships should be addressed c/o the Fleet Mail Office, Bombay.  The No., rank and other 
particulars of the addressee, as well as the name of the ship, should be indicated.
```
