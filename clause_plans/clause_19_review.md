## Poora clause (PDF se)
```
19. Posting in Special Letter Boxes  – Letter Boxes marked ‘For letters only’ should be 
used for the posting only of letters and postcards.  Similarly, the special letter boxes provided in 
the cities for posting of Air Mail Articles QMS  articles be used only for posting such classes of 
articles.  Other articles if posted in such special letter -boxes, are liable to detention.  QMS articles 
should bear full PIN below the name of the town of destination.
```
