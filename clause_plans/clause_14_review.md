## Poora clause (PDF se)
```
14. Fictitious Stamps . –  The manufacture and use of fictitious postage stamps for any 
purpose whatsoever is prohibited, and is an offence punishab le under Section 263-A of the Indian 
Penal Code.  Reproduction of stamps is, however, allowed for illustration purposes in a philatelic 
publication or in an article or section relating wholly to postage stamps which may appear in a 
publication or a general character.  Such productions, however, must only be in black. 
 
 
GENERAL RULES AS TO POSTING
```
