🛡 PLI / RPLI: key FAQs

  • Advantage: low premium + high bonus · tax benefit under 80C · pay at any PO or the online portal · toll free 1800 266 6868
  • Lapse: 6 unpaid premiums for a policy under 3 years, 12 for older ones · revival any number of times if 5 consecutive years haven't passed · dues + 12% interest + health certificate · up to 12 instalments
  • Missed one month → pay next month with a 1% fine
  • IMPNo medical needed: up to ₹2 lakh at any age · PLI up to ₹5 lakh (age ≤ 40) · RPLI up to ₹1 lakh (age ≤ 35) · non-medical policies: deduction on death claim within 3 years
  • Loan: WLA and CWLA after 4 years (EA after 3) · not in AEA (Sumangal) and children's policy · interest 10%, half-yearly
  • Surrender: WLA, EA, CWLA, Yugal Suraksha after 3 years · not in AEA and children's policy · bonus on reduced sum only if surrendered after 5 years
  • Advance premium rebate: PLI 6 months 1%, 12 months 2% · RPLI 3 months 0.5%, 6 months 1%, 12 months 2% · ₹1 a month rebate per ₹20,000 sum assured
  • Only one spouse eligible → Yugal Suraksha · always nominate · death claim rejected → appeal to the next higher authority
  • Eligible: central / state employees, GDS, defence, paramilitary, PSUs, banks, educational institutions, doctors, engineers, CAs, lawyers, employees of NSE / BSE listed companies · RPLI = all rural residents

80C benefit: 5-year TD, SCSS, PPF, SSA, NSC · SB interest under 80TTA (up to ₹10,000)

1 Savings Account (SB)

  • Single adult · joint (2–3) · minor above 10 in own name · guardian for a minor / person of unsound mind
  • One single account per person · single ↔ joint conversion not allowed · nomination mandatory
  • Minimum ₹500, then not less than ₹10 · below ₹500 at year end → ₹50 maintenance fee, closed if it hits zero
  • Interest: on the minimum balance from the 10th to month end, credited at year end · 80TTA exemption up to ₹10,000
  • No transaction for 3 financial years → silent account · revived with KYC
  • Cheque book, ATM, net / mobile banking (within DOP only) · cheque bounce ₹50

2 Recurring Deposit (RD)

  • Minimum ₹100 a month, multiples of ₹10 · opened 1st–15th → pay by the 15th each month · opened after 16th → by the last working day
  • Default: ₹1 per ₹100 per month · 4 defaults → discontinued, revivable within 2 months · with up to 4 defaults the term can be extended by those months
  • Advance: up to 5 years · on a ₹100 account, 6 instalments → ₹10 rebate, 12 → ₹40
  • IMPLoan: after 12 instalments + 1 year, 50% of the balance · interest = RD rate + 2%
  • Premature closure: after 3 years (SB rate) · extendable 5 more years (original rate) · can be kept 5 years without deposits
  • On death the nominee may continue it till maturity

3 Time Deposit (TD)

  • Minimum ₹1000, multiples of ₹100, no maximum · interest yearly (no interest on unclaimed interest)
  • 5-year TD → 80C · extension: 1-year within 6 months, 2-year within 12 months, 3 / 5-year within 18 months of maturity
  • Premature: after 6 months · closed after 1 year → 2% less than that term's rate
  • IMPA 5-year TD opened on / after 10-11-2023 can't be closed for 4 years · can be pledged (banks, RBI, co-operatives, govt companies)

4 Monthly Income Scheme (MIS)

  • Minimum ₹1000 (multiples of ₹1000) · max ₹9 lakh single, ₹15 lakh joint (equal shares) · a minor's limit is separate
  • Interest every month, no interest on unclaimed interest · auto credit to SB / ECS
  • No closure before 1 year · 1–3 years → 2% cut from principal · 3–5 years → 1% · full at 5 years

5 Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS)

  • IMPAge 60+ · retired civilians 55–60 · retired defence 50–60 (within 1 month of retirement benefits)
  • Single or jointly with the spouse only · the whole joint deposit belongs to the first holder
  • Minimum ₹1000 · maximum ₹30 lakh · 80C · interest quarterly · TDS if interest exceeds ₹50,000 (not with 15G / 15H)
  • Close any time: before 1 year → no interest · 1–2 years → 1.5% cut · 2–5 years → 1% · after 5 years extend by 3 years

6 Public Provident Fund (PPF)

  • One account in the whole country (PO or bank) · ₹500 to ₹1.5 lakh a year, multiples of ₹50 · 80C · interest at year end
  • No ₹500 in a year → discontinued · revive: ₹500 + ₹50 per defaulted year · no loan / withdrawal on discontinued accounts
  • Loan: after 1 year from the end of the opening year, before 5 years · 25% of the balance at the end of the second preceding year · repaid within 36 months → 1% interest, later → 6%
  • Withdrawal: after 5 years, once a year, up to 50%
  • Matures after 15 financial years · extend in blocks of 5 years within 1 year (with deposits, up to 60% withdrawal)
  • Premature closure (after 5 years): serious illness, higher education, becoming an NRI → 1% less interest · on death it closes, the nominee can not continue it

7 Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSA)

  • Girl below 10, opened by the guardian · one account per girl · max 2 girls per family (exception for twins / triplets)
  • Minimum ₹250, max ₹1.5 lakh a year · deposits for 15 years · default → revive with ₹250 + ₹50 per year · 80C
  • Guardian operates till 18 · 50% withdrawal after 18 or passing 10th UP 2020 (once a year, max 5 times)
  • Closure: at 21 years · or on marriage after 18 · after 5 years: death / extreme compassion

8–11 NSC, KVP, MSSC, PM CARES

  • NSC (VIII): single or up to 3 adults joint · from ₹1000, no limit · 5 years · 80C · can be pledged · no early closure (except death or forfeiture)
  • KVP: from ₹1000, no limit · maturity set by the govt (doubles in 115 months) · closure: on death or after 2.5 years · can be pledged
  • Mahila Samman Savings Certificate (MSSC): ₹1000 to ₹2 lakh · 3-month gap between two accounts · 7.5%, compounded quarterly · 40% withdrawal after 1 year · closable after 6 months without reason · matures in 2 years
  • PM CARES for Children 2021: children who lost parents to COVID (11.03.2020 to 28.02.2022) · DM as joint holder · corpus ₹10 lakh at 18 · monthly stipend from MIS interest till 23 · ₹10 lakh paid at 23

📱 ePassbook, claims, ATM

  • ePassbook: via OTP · balance + mini statement (SB, PPF, SSA) · full statement in phases
  • Death claim: with nomination → form + death certificate · without nomination, up to ₹5 lakh → affidavits etc., 6 months after death · above ₹5 lakh → legal evidence (succession certificate etc.)
  • Account transfer: form SB-10(B) · duplicate certificate → as a passbook, from the HO (no indemnity bond for mutilated ones)
  • ATM: ₹25,000 a day, ₹10,000 per transaction · free at other banks: 3 in metros, 5 elsewhere, then ₹20 + GST · card replacement ₹300 · annual ₹125 · SMS ₹12
