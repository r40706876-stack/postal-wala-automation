🏛 Organisation of the Department of Posts (DOP)

  • Started by Lord Dalhousie · present form from 1 October 1854
  • IMPDOP comes under the Ministry of Communications · in charge = Communications Minister Bihar 2021
  • Administrative control → DG Post, New Delhi (all administration + operations)
  • Head of DOP → Secretary, Department of Posts
  • Headquarters → Dak Bhawan, New Delhi
  • PLI Directorate → New Delhi · PLI Director → Kolkata
  • IMPWhole country = 23 postal circles Chhattisgarh 2021 · Delhi 2022 · Telangana 2020
  • A separate wing for the armed forces, the Army Postal Service (APS) = Base Circle
  • Office of the Controller of Foreign Mails → Mumbai

Clause 3 Night Post Office

  • IMPNormal working hours fixed by → Head of Circle (CPMG) Maharashtra 2020 · Bihar 2021
  • DG can extend any PO's hours up to 8:30 PM + keep it open on Sunday = Night PO Chhattisgarh 2021
  • A Night PO does only the work authorised by the DG
  • In extended hours: registered booking (VP included) · IPO + stamp sale · SB deposit + cash certificates (till 7 PM)
  • Sunday / holiday: 10 AM to 5 PM
  • Stopped on Sunday / holiday: delivery · SB + PO certificates · MO payment
DG = "Door open at niGht" · DG stretches the time
DG extends hours, CPMG fixes normal hours. On Sunday it is 10–5, not 8:30.

Clause 4 Mobile Post Office

  • A specially designed van going to fixed points → late-posting facility in cities
  • Stamps + stationery · insured parcels · surface + air registered letter mail · air parcels · unregistered mail · e-MO · My Stamp · postal banking
  • No VP booking · closed on Sundays and postal holidays
  • MO booking allowed only at mobile POs of Madras and Nagpur
Mobile: no VP · MO only in Ma-Na (Madras, Nagpur)
The mobile PO is also closed on Sunday. Only the Night PO is open on Sunday.

Clause 9 Why prepay postage

  • Fully prepaid articles → go by the earliest mail + are delivered fast
  • Unpaid / underpaid: no extra security · may be detained to tax the postage due · not issued with special deliveries
Full stamp = full speed 🚀

Clause 10 Postage stamps and stationery

  • Postage is normally paid with postage stamps · issued by India Post under the authority of the Government of India
  • Stationery (envelopes, inland letter cards, postcards) has the stamp embossed · if more postage is needed, add extra stamps
  • Postage can also be paid by franking machine impressions
  • Overseas philately orders → Indian Philatelic Bureau, Bombay GPO
Revenue stamp: the PO only sells it, the Central Government issues it, and it can not be used for postage. MP 2020

Franking Key points

  • Machine number X123456: X = maker (F Francotyp Postalia, N Neopost, P Pitney Bowes) + 6 digits
  • Funds kept safe in the PSD (Postal Security Device) · maker's server dials the DOP server 3 times a day
  • New licence 14 working days · renewal 5 working days (start 1 month before expiry) · rejected → fee back in 30 working days
  • Bring: window delivery ticket (identification) + 2 copies of the SOM · 3% commission on value of franks
  • Govt departments: advance ₹2000 to ₹9900 · receipt ACG-67 · 2 offices incl. 1 HO (LSG or above) · word "Service" in the licence die · registers kept 2 years

Clause 12 Prepayment of postage in cash

  • For firms posting very large numbers of unregistered packets → certain POs authorised by the Head of Circle
  • Minimum: 500 in big cities · 250 in small towns at a time
  • Some POs in Bombay, Kolkata, Madras, Nagpur, Delhi → registration fee also in cash (with special registered journals)
  • Selected gazetted / HSG POs → firms posting at least 500 articles
  • Available for: letters, ILCs, postcards, packets, unregistered articles, registered newspapers Chhattisgarh 2022 · not for others
  • Special registration envelopes (from the PO) → registered articles only

Clauses 13–14 Spoilt and fictitious stamps

  • Not valid for postage: cut, torn, defaced, obliterated, over-printed, imperfect stamps, or stamps cut from embossed envelopes
  • Re-using a used stamp to cause loss to the government = offence under the IPC
  • IMPMaking / using fictitious stamps = punishable under Section 263-A, IPC 1860 Tamil Nadu 2020
  • Reproduction allowed for illustration in philatelic publications, only in black
263-A = fictitious stamps · philatelic reproduction = black
500 packets in big cities, 250 in small towns. Don't swap them.

Clauses 15–16 Packing and sealing

  • IMPEvery letter / packet / parcel gets date stamps of at least 2 POs: booking + destination Rajasthan · Jharkhand · Andhra 2021 · Bihar · MP · Delhi 2022
  • Strong cover: pressure + friction in mail bags + stamping pressure
  • The PO takes no special precaution for fragile articles
  • Wax cloth → inside cover, strong paper outside (for date stamps and stamps) · no sharp edges
  • Avoid sealing wax outside unregistered articles · if used, lay thin tissue paper over it · especially for foreign mail through hot climates

Clause 17 Coins and valuables

Coins, bullion, precious stones, jewellery, gold / silver articles, currency notes → inland only insured Gujarat 2020

  • "Gold or silver articles" include partly made of them (not coins)
  • "Coins" exclude cut counterfeit coins sent by the currency department · "notes" exclude cancelled cut notes
  • "Jewellery" includes watches with gold / silver cases

Clauses 18–20 Letter boxes

  • Letters, postcards, ILCs, packets → letter boxes at POs, public places, mobile POs, steamers
  • IMPMachine-franked articles not in letter boxes Bihar 2021 · 2022 · Delhi 2022
  • "Letters only" boxes → only letters · other articles in air mail / QMS boxes are detained
  • QMS articles need the full PIN below the town name
  • Large letters that don't fit → at the window · bulk letters too, if postage + late fee are paid
