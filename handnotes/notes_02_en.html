Clauses 20–22 Articles handed in at the window

  • All parcels, whatever the weight → at the counter of a PO / mail office
  • Registered newspapers + franked articles → at the counter named in the licence
  • IMPRegistered / insured / VP / certificate-of-posting articles → at least half an hour before the mail closes
  • Mark clearly above the address: "Registered" / "Insured for ₹..." / "Value Payable for ₹..."
  • Air mail with high-value adhesive stamps → at the window, stamps defaced in the presence of the person presenting it
  • Posting just as the mail leaves → congestion + delay. Post early (Clause 22)

Clauses 23–25 How to affix stamps

  • IMPStamps → right-hand top corner of the address side Rajasthan 2021 · Maharashtra 2022 · Chhattisgarh 2021
  • Stamps must stick fully to the article itself, not to its contents or a separate label
  • Non-postal stamps, charity stamps, labels, seals → not on the address side (make the address hard to read)
  • No impressions that look like a franking machine impression
  • Use the minimum number of stamps (Clause 25) → many small stamps take space + add work for staff

✉ How to write the address

  • Wrong / incomplete address = delay or wrong delivery · it must be complete enough to deliver without enquiry
  • Address parallel to the length of the cover, in the lower half, towards the right
  • Leave at least 3 cm clear at the top (stamps, labels, postmarks)
  • Post town in block letters, approved spelling · foreign addresses in Roman letters
  • Two post towns with the same name → add the district · district and town same name → add the state
  • IMPSender's address → lower left-hand corner or on the back Chhattisgarh 2021 · MP 2020
  • Concession / free articles (Book post, Blind literature) → write the class at the top of the address side
  • Bulky letter that may look like another class → write "Letter" on it
Town address (order)
Name → designation / profession → house no. + street → locality → post town + PIN → mobile
Village address (order)
Name → father's / husband's name → house no. → village + thana → PO of delivery + PIN → taluka, district, state (not needed with the right PIN) → mobile

Machine-sortable envelopes (standard): receiver's address on the front, sender's on the back · 15 mm blank on left, bottom, right · address block 40 mm below the top · stamps / frank 10 mm from the address · 15 mm at the bottom for the barcode · logo max 25 × 50 mm

The President and certain high officers get mail even on tour (list in Clause 206) → address such articles "Camp", not a post town.

📦 Packing special items

  • Parcels in a strong case / wrapper · cloth / woollens → strong wrapper + outer cover · wooden or stout cardboard box is best
  • Live bees → in cases that can't hurt other mail or staff
  • Liquids → double receptacle: bottle inside, metal / strong wooden box outside, saw-dust / bran in between (to absorb)
  • Human viscera → may go to Chemical Examiners · brains of rabid animals to authorised labs · glass bottle, tight stopper (wax if alcohol), in a wooden / tin box with 18 mm cotton, cloth outside, seals every 3 inches
  • Plague cultures → only from authorised doctors / with govt permission, to a govt lab · hermetically closed + 18 mm layer + declaration
  • Osmic acid → sealed glass capsule, in a tin of sand (at least 4.3 cm), wooden box outside + 2.5 cm cotton · red letters "OSMIC ACID" · max 2 grams per capsule
  • Perfumes → plugged bottle, cardboard box, then wooden box · red letters "Perfumes - keep away from fire"
Liquid = "bottle inside a box" · osmic acid = 2 grams · viscera / cultures = 18 mm cotton

Clauses 27–31 Army, navy, addresses

  • IMPArmy / Air Force personnel → PIN of 56 APO or 99 APO, no post town Maharashtra 2022
  • IMPNavy personnel on INS ships → C/o Fleet Mail Office, Bombay + number, rank, ship's name Punjab 2021 · Chhattisgarh 2022 · Bihar 2021 · MP 2020 · Odisha 2021
  • Indefinite address = not delivered: addressed to a class of people, only initials / figures / fictitious names · unless in care of a definite person or place (house, post box, street number)
  • Registered / insured / VP / MO → only to a definite person or firm
  • Sender's name + address (with PIN) in the lower left corner → returned unopened if not delivered (Clause 30)
  • Many undelivered articles are destroyed every year in RLOs · for photographs in packets write the address on the back too
  • When giving your address to people abroad, always mention the state (Clause 31)

📮 Post box: other points

  • Articles "C/o Post Box" are not put in the box, except for: the firm's staff, family / guests, trainees of an institution, visitors staying with the renter
  • Several renters may share one box, but each pays a separate fee · no right to a particular number / row
  • Wrong information or misuse → box withdrawn without notice
  • Key lost → pay for the key only · lock (or both) lost → pay for both · not reporting = allotment cancelled
  • Delivery ticket = same number as the box, not transferable · clearance may be stopped up to half an hour before each delivery
  • Article too big for the box → intimation slip inside, collect at the window · too much mail → extra box with the same number

📭 Letter box attendant (LB peon)

  • IMPMain duty = clearing the letter boxes of his beat on time Tamil Nadu 2020 · Haryana 2020 · Andhra 2020
  • Stamping, sealing, unloading mail · reports damaged boxes to the postmaster · knows clearance and mail times
  • Gets keys + hour plates + a bag from the postmaster before leaving · returns them at once · keys always kept by the postmaster
  • May carry mail to town SOs / BOs, work as packers · no MO payments other than TMOs
  • Nayatha software + PMA app: scan the barcode inside the box → GPRS to the server, GPS checked
  • Who clears: GDS DA / MTS / Postman · office boxes → BPM in a BO, GDS DA / MTS / Mail PA in SOs / HOs

Section III Official postal articles (Clauses 198–207) Bihar 2021

  • Same rates and rules as non-official mail · exceptions: "Service Unpaid" postcards, parcels with official files
  • Department of Posts officers, Postal Audit officers → departmental mail free
  • Superscription OIGS (On India Government Service) or On Service + sender's signature + full designation (lower left)
  • Service postcards already carry "Service" → nothing more needed · known abbreviations may be accepted, but no one can claim it
  • IMPUnpaid official article → charged the prepaid rate on delivery · underpaid → only the deficiency Chhattisgarh 2021 · Andhra 2020 · Kerala 2021
  • Prepayment on parcels is compulsory · an official parcel with dues goes back to the sender
  • Entitled to service stamps: Central, State, local bodies, judiciary · authorised by the DG (later changes: Head of Circle) · officials on leave / retired: no
  • A postal ballot = an official postal article
  • Mail of local fund / local authority officers = not official (can't use OIGS)
  • Ordinary stamps used instead of service stamps → the PO in charge reports it to the sending office · repeated → to the CPMG
  • Govt officer → private person: official, prepaid · exceptions: certified court copies, govt books → "Service Unpaid", charged on delivery
  • Govt officers must pay postage due on articles addressed to them · not with service stamps

Camp mail (Clauses 205–206)

  • High constitutional / govt officers on tour → their mail in a special registered mail bag (Camp Correspondence)
  • Only fully prepaid, uninsured letter mail · addressed "Camp", no post town · bags delivered on Sundays + holidays too
  • Parcels, MOs, insured, VP with dues → not in special bags · no redirection in transit
  • Camp bag (files): as parcel → max 10 kg, postage of a 10 kg parcel + registration + ₹16 · as air parcel → + ₹6 · as letter mail → registration fee only · 2 labels (address + tag)
  • Tour programme given in time to the Department (Council of Ministers → DG Post, New Delhi) · changes to them + the local superintendent

Section IV Prohibited articles (Clause 209) Haryana 2020

  • Anything sent against any law in force
  • Indecent paintings, photos, books, cards Andhra 2021
  • Indecent, seditious, threatening words on the cover
  • Explosive, inflammable, dangerous, filthy things · matches count as explosives
  • Inflammable liquids: flash point below 200°F · spirits 90°F or below · turpentine · all varnishes
  • Harmful living creatures
  • IMPLottery tickets / adverts MP 2020 · govt lotteries exempt (declaration + name and address)
  • Newspapers not following the Press & Registration of Books Act 1867
  • IMPGold coins / bullion / ornaments over ₹1 lakh (earlier ₹20,000) Kerala 2021
  • Coins, currency notes (DG may allow for specific purposes)
  • Address side split into several parts (more than one address)
  • Dangerous goods, narcotics, items banned by IATA / ICAO / wildlife · batteries banned by air → surface only

Don't use pins to join reply postcards or packet covers (they injure other mail).

Local prohibitions (Clause 210)

  • Anywhere in India: opium, morphia, coca leaves, bhang, ganja, charas, cocaine → prohibited · exempt: recognised medicines, govt consignments, licensed chemists (permit + declaration)
  • Skins / feathers of birds other than domestic birds → prohibited (exempt: ostrich feathers, natural history specimens to / from Port Blair)
  • Kutch out of J&K → only with a permit from the Conservator of Forests, Kashmir
  • Pests / disease: plants with San Jose scale from several states · Icerya purchasi from TN, Kerala, Karnataka · bananas from Maharashtra, Kerala, Odisha (Bunchy top disease)

Injurious and suspect articles (Clauses 211–213)

  • Suspected injurious → postmaster detains it and refers to the Head of Circle
  • Articles with pins: found at the booking PO → returned at once · found later → returned through the RLO, pins removed
  • Goods against the law → returned to the sender · only suspected → forwarded marked "Doubtful", opened in the presence of the addressee
  • Imported gold / jewellery → only after customs duty is paid
  • Matter against law and order, harmful to a community, or a picture of a proclaimed offender → not sent, if found → RLO
