👵 Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

  • Announced in the 2015-16 budget · run by PFRDA through the NPS set-up
  • IMPAge 18–40 · one account per person · pension amount can be changed once a year
  • Govt co-contribution: 50% or ₹1000 a year (whichever is lower) → for non-taxpayers who joined 1 June–31 Dec 2015
  • Late payment penalty: ₹1 per month for every ₹100
  • Exit at 60 with pension · on death → pension to the spouse → after both, corpus to the nominee
  • No exit before 60, except on death or terminal illness
  • PMJJBY: cover ends at age 55 · even with many accounts, cover limited to ₹2 lakh

🏢 For bulk customers

BNPL (Book Now Pay Later)

  • Contract customers of Speed Post / Business Post · bulk customer = at least ₹10,000 of Speed Post a month
  • Approval: Director / Chief PM in GPOs, Divisional Head elsewhere · registration within 2 days · below ₹10,000 → CPMG / PMG, 7 days
  • Booking at many locations: ₹1 lakh a month · National Account Facility: across circles, ₹1 crore a year
  • 3-year agreement → bank guarantee valid 39 months · renewed 2 years at a time
  • Bill monthly, by the 7th · pay by the month's last day, or a 12% penalty

COD (Cash on Delivery)

  • Contract customers of Business Parcel, Express Parcel, Speed Post · up to ₹50,000 collected from the addressee
  • Insurance not compulsory · no coupons / Snowball schemes · delivered to anyone at the address on payment

Bill Mail Service (BMS)

  • Bills, account statements · at least once in 90 days · at least 5000 articles at a time
  • Postage: up to 50 g ₹3 + ₹2 per 50 g · "Bill Mail Service" in bold at the top middle · hand over PIN-sorted
  • Within a city / district · adverts allowed, but not a purely publicity item
  • NBMS (national): anywhere in India · first class, by air

📢 Direct, Media, Business, Logistics Post

  • IMPDirect Post = unaddressed mail (leaflets, brochures, samples, CDs) MP 2022 · started 2 June 2005 · at least 1000 · not in letter boxes, in bulk · 5% discount above 50,000
  • Media Post = advertising through the post (postcards, ILCs, MO forms, mail vans, PO buildings) · apply to the Divisional Head, licence from the Circle Head · aerogrammes in English only · 4 colours · min 10 lakh (multi-colour) / 5 lakh (single colour)
  • Business Post = from mail preparation to delivery (collecting, franking, inserting, sealing) · Business Post Centres · annual reports on cash prepayment (special licence)
  • Logistics Post = collection → storage → transmission → distribution · no upper weight limit · minimum chargeable 50 kg (multiples of 10 kg) · FTL / LTL · road, rail, air
  • Logistics: door to door ₹1 / kg (min ₹100) · airport ₹1 / kg (min ₹50) · unloading free up to 500 kg, then ₹1000 per ton

💻 e-Payment, e-Post, Retail Post

  • e-Payment = many people paying one organisation (electricity, phone bills, fees, taxes) · 14,000+ POs · to become a biller, apply to the CPMG · MIS with 5 fields
  • e-Post = message over the internet, printed and delivered · retail ₹10 per A4 page + GST · corporate ₹6 (₹5 for 50+ messages)
  • Retail Post = third-party services at POs: bills, taxes, forms, railway tickets · network of 1.5 lakh+ POs

Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB)

  • 1 unit = 1 gram of gold · 2.5% interest on top of the gold price rise, paid half-yearly
  • Tenure 8 years, exit from the 5th year · min 1 g · max 4 kg (individual / HUF), 20 kg (trusts)
  • Residents of India only · tradable on stock exchanges · no capital gains tax on redemption

🛡 PLI / RPLI · Mind map

PLI
started 1 Feb 1884 Bihar 2020 · MP 2022
max ₹50 lakh Chhattisgarh · Assam 2021
Suraksha (Whole Life)
Paid at 80 or on death · loan after 4 years · surrender after 3 years · convertible to Endowment up to 59 · premium till 55 / 58 / 60
Suvidha (Convertible)
Whole Life, convert to Endowment after 5, before 6 years · if not, stays Whole Life · loan after 4 years
Santosh (Endowment)
Paid at a chosen age: 35, 40, 45, 50, 55, 58, 60 · loan after 3 years · surrender before 5 years = no bonus
Yugal Suraksha
Husband and wife, one premium · age 21–45 · loan / surrender after 3 years
Sumangal (Money Back)
15 yrs: 20% each at 6, 9, 12, then 40% · 20 yrs: 20% each at 8, 12, 16, then 40% · age 19 to 40 / 45
Bal Jeevan Bima
Max 2 children, age 5–20 · parent under 45 · PLI ₹3 lakh, RPLI ₹1 lakh (or the parent's, whichever is less) · no premium after parent's death · no medical

🌾 PLI and RPLI: other points

  • PLI began for postal employees · 1894: female employees too (when no other insurer covered women) · now central / state govt, defence, PSUs, banks, teachers, doctors, engineers, CAs, lawyers etc.
  • IMPRPLI started 24.03.1995 · on the Malhotra Committee (1993) recommendation MP 2022 · then only 22% insured, life insurance = 10% of household savings
  • Aim: rural people, weaker sections, rural women workers
  • RPLI also has: Gram Suraksha, Gram Suvidha, Gram Santosh, Gram Sumangal (max ₹10 lakh), Bal Jeevan Bima · RPLI (Gram Suraksha) max ₹10 lakh Assam 2022
  • Gram Priya (RPLI only): 10 years · 20% at 4 years, 20% at 7, 60% + bonus at 10 · ₹10,000 to ₹10 lakh · no interest on arrears up to 1 year in floods / disasters
  • RPLI loan: after 3 years (Endowment) Kerala 2021
  • PLI money back policy = Sumangal UP 2020 · farmers are not eligible for PLI (they go under RPLI) Delhi 2022 · Bihar 2021
