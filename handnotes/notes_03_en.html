1–3 Letters, ILC, postcards

Letter

  • Any communication closed in an envelope · max weight 2 kg · postage first 20 g ₹5 + ₹5 per extra 20 g
  • Size: min 14 × 9 cm · any one side max 60 cm · length + breadth + depth ≤ 90 cm · roll: diameter 10–80 cm, length + 2×diameter 17–100 cm
  • Flap at the back on the longer side · paper 0.08–0.18 mm, at least 70 GSM, bends into a "U" · no metal clips / staples
  • Unpaid letter → double the postage · underpaid → double the deficiency

Inland Letter Card (ILC)

  • Price ₹2.50 · max weight 5 grams Chhattisgarh 2021
  • Unfolded max 30 × 21 cm (min 28.2 × 18.2) · folded max 21 × 10 cm (min 15.2 × 9)
  • Unpaid → double · underpaid → double the deficiency · breaking the rules → taxed as a letter
  • Private ILCs allowed (DOP sizes) · not for foreign countries, except Pakistan and Nepal

Postcard

  • Size 14 × 9 cm Andhra 2021 · private cards just as thick
  • Nothing can be attached to a postcard Punjab 2021
  • Meghdoot: advertisement in 4 colours next to the address
  • Competition card: for TV / radio / newspaper contests (now discontinued)

4–8 Book packets, blind literature, samples

  • IMPBook packet max 5 kg Chhattisgarh 2021 · size min 10 × 7 cm, max 60 × 30 × 30 cm (roll 10–80 cm)
  • Postage: first 50 g ₹4 + ₹3 per 50 g · air surcharge ₹2 for the first 50 g, then ₹1
  • No personal / written communication MP 2020 · no money, stamps, cheques · a card headed "Postcard" can't go as a book packet
  • Allowed: greetings, invitations, circulars, horoscopes, pictures, maps · newspapers, printed music · hand-drawn business / legal documents
  • Court summons / notices → not as book packets
  • Rules broken → charged letter or parcel postage (whichever applies) · over size / weight → returned · unpaid → double
  • Printed books (no adverts, e.g. textbooks) → ₹1 per 100 g, 5 kg · mark "Printed Books"
  • Periodicals → registered with the RNI, price printed on the cover
  • Sample / pattern → bona fide trade samples · max 2 kg · other rules as book packets
  • IMPBlind literature → max 7 kg Bihar 2021 · Delhi 2022 · Chhattisgarh 2021 · postage + registration + AD fees free · free only by surface · marked "Literature for the Blind" · in Braille, ordinary type only for title / contents

9 Registered newspapers (RNP)

  • News, published in large numbers at intervals of not more than 31 days · follow the RNI + Press & Registration Act 1867
  • Registered by the area's SP / SSP (or an independent gazetted postmaster) · "Registered" + number on the first / last page
  • Number format: DN/XYZ/2024-2026 (DN = division, XYZ = number, 3-year block) Andhra 2021
  • First registration valid till 31 December of the third calendar year · each renewal for 3 years
  • No registration fee · renewal ₹50 (after 30 Sep, before 31 Dec) · after expiry ₹100
  • List of at least 50 subscribers · at least 100 copies at a time · max 5 kg
  • Postage: single copy first 50 g ₹0.25, 50–100 g ₹0.50, ₹0.20 per extra 100 g · packet up to 100 g ₹0.50 + ₹0.20

10 Parcels

  • Anything except prohibited articles · may contain one personal letter to the addressee
  • Any other written communication → double letter postage each · suspected → "for open delivery", opened before the addressee · if unpaid, recovered from the sender
  • IMPWeight: parcel max 20 kg · registered parcel from / to a BO 10 kg Maharashtra 2020 · Chhattisgarh 2021 · Kerala 2021 · Delhi 2022 · unregistered parcel 4 kg Odisha · Punjab 2021 · Kerala 2020
  • Postage: first 500 g ₹19 + ₹16 per 500 g · home delivery charge above 5 kg
  • IMPParcel found in a letter box → treated and charged as a registered parcel Andhra 2021
  • International parcel lost: 40 SDR per parcel + 4.50 SDR per kg

🔒 Registration

  • Registered articles pass with special care, but the PO is not liable for loss / damage
  • Can be registered: letters, ILCs, book / pattern packets, blind literature, RNP, postcards, parcels · postage + fee prepaid · at the window, receipt given
  • IMPRegistration compulsory: insured articles Bihar 2021 · Delhi 2022 · MP 2022 · parcels needing a customs declaration · articles with stamps / cheques / hundis / notes · VP articles
  • Fees: registration ₹17 · AD ₹3 · from 01-11-2023, 18% GST on (postage + registration)
  • Compensation (an act of grace, not legal liability): Head of Circle up to ₹100 · international registered 30 SDR
  • Copy of the receipt signed by the addressee within 6 months (₹2)

🛡 Insurance

  • All charges (postage + registration + insurance) prepaid · stamps spaced apart so they don't hide damage
  • Insured value ≤ real value · gold, coins, notes → insured for the actual value
  • IMPTo / from Army Post Offices: max ₹500
  • Compulsory for: coins, bullion, precious stones, gold / silver, notes · VP amount above ₹1500 (except papers / books of no intrinsic value)
  • Packing: strong cover, identical wax seals on every flap (private mark, not a coin) · no black / coloured borders or transparent windows · gold → wooden / metal case
  • At the window · value in words and figures, no corrections · sender's address bottom left · AD free
  • Compensation paid 1 month after the loss is reported · never more than the value lost
No compensation when: wrong / incomplete address · fraud · addressee signed the receipt · loss not reported within 3 months · poor packing · no visible damage to cover / seals · notes / gold not insured for actual value · halves of notes · damage from the nature of the article · prohibited contents

💰 VPP (Value Payable Post)

  • The addressee pays when taking delivery; the money goes to the sender by MO
  • Can go VP: registered parcels, letters, book packets, RNP · not "Snowball" (coupon / ticket) schemes
  • Declaration: sent on a bona fide order · documents of no intrinsic value allowed (railway receipts, bills, policies)
  • Only MO office to MO office · amount in the form · on the article: "VP" + amount at the upper left, sender's address lower left
  • Bulk VP: at least 10 uninsured VP articles · enquire with the local SPOs
  • IMPNot taken within 7 days → returned on the 8th day UP 2020 · Chhattisgarh 2021 · written request + fee for 7 more days (fee not refunded)
  • On return the sender pays any dues · prepaid fees are never refunded
  • Complaints within 6 months, a fee per article (refunded if right) · suspected fraud → Head of Circle may withhold payment
  • Govt is liable only once the amount is received within 6 months · insured value and VP amount can differ (a repaired watch)

⚡ Speed Post

  • The Department's flagship product · rate by distance + weight · up to 50 g anywhere ₹35, local ₹15 (taxes extra)
  • IMPMax weight 35 kg Kerala 2020 · Haryana 2022 · Chhattisgarh 2022 · MP 2022 · insurance up to ₹1 lakh
  • Tracking: 13-character number on indiapost.gov.in or the "Post Info" app
  • Compensation: double the charges or ₹1000 (whichever is less) · treated as lost after 60 days from booking or 30 days from complaint (whichever is later)
  • Contract customers: money back guarantee, BNPL, COD up to ₹50,000, discounts (+1% with advance deposit, +2% above ₹25 lakh a month)
  • Delivery in a BO takes 1 extra day · size: one side or length + girth not over 150 cm
  • Treated as a Speed Post parcel if: over 500 g · larger than 38 × 27 × 2 cm · or declared "Merchandise"

Business Parcel

Contract customers · premium surface service · free pick-up, BNPL, COD ₹50,000 · insurance optional · uninsured loss: ₹500 or actual value (whichever is less)

🌍 International mail

  • Foreign mail is processed at Offices of Exchange: Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, Kochi
  • Services: letters, postcards, aerogrammes, small packets, blind literature, printed papers, M Bag (newspapers / books for one addressee)
  • Registration: letters, small packets, printed papers → ₹150 per article · registered M Bag lost: 150 SDR
  • EMS (International Speed Post): max 35 kg · one side 1.5 m, length + girth 3 m · merchandise lost: value or 130 SDR · delay over 5 days → 5% of postage
  • International parcel: length 1.05 m, length + girth 2 m · min surface 90 × 140 mm · customs declaration required · compensation 40 SDR + 4.50 SDR / kg, max 130 SDR
  • SAL (Surface Air Lifted) = surface inside the country, air between countries
  • Paid for delivering inward mail: EMS merchandise min 5 SDR · parcels 7 SDR
  • International Tracked Packet → e-commerce, to the Asia-Pacific region Bihar 2020 · compensation ₹1000 or actual value
