📤 Exports by post

  • Started at 5 FPOs (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Kochi) → now exports from 28 locations
  • An exporter with a valid IEC (Import-Export Code) → files a Postal Bill of Export (Export by Post Regulations 2018)
  • PBE-I = e-commerce exports · PBE-II = other commercial goods · customs declarations CN 22 / CN 23
  • Natural persons (not firms) → no PBE needed
  • Products: registered packets (2 kg), air parcels (20 kg), International Tracked Packet, EMS

💸 Money order rules

  • MO form 25 paise (not adjusted in the commission) · form + amount + commission at the window in cash / cheque · receipt given
  • PO not responsible for: wrong payment due to wrong details from the remitter · accidental delay / refusal · wrong payment after 1 year from issue
  • Acknowledgement of payment sent to the remitter · for e-MO it stays in the HO and is sent on demand
  • Illiterate payee → puts his mark before a witness who signs
  • Payee moved and left redirection instructions → redirected free
  • Changing the address / PO → free · IMPchanging the payee's name → second commission (equal to the first) · stopping payment → free
  • Payee refuses → returned to the remitter at once, free · on request / payee not found → detained max 7 days
  • Can't be paid or repaid → void, amount credited to the Central Govt · paid later if applied within 1 year
  • The DG can suspend MO issue at any PO (except MOs at a higher special commission)

My Stamp

  • Stamp sheets with your photo / logo · one sheet = 12 stamps × ₹5 (sheet ₹300) Bihar 2021 · Delhi 2022
  • Corporates: at least 100 sheets, 10% off the first 100 + 20% beyond · collect within 7 working days
  • Customised My Stamp: at least 5000 sheets, 20% discount (₹12 lakh total) · India Post prints 1000 extra · orders can't change, no refunds

Deen Dayal SPARSH Yojana + PAC

  • SPARSH = philately scholarship · total 920 · max 40 per circle (10 each from classes VI, VII, VIII, IX)

  • PAC (Philatelic Advisory Committee): chaired by the Minister for Communications, co-chaired by the MoS · 1 MP each from Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha + eminent philatelists

👴 NPS (National Pension System)

  • Voluntary retirement savings · regulator PFRDA · CRA = NSDL · DOP = a POP (Point of Presence), at all HOs
  • Age 18–70, Indian citizens (NRIs too) · NRI / OCI: Tier-I only · mandatory for govt employees (per the notes, except WB, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh), optional for others
  • Benefits: world's lowest-cost pension scheme · simple · flexible (choose your fund) · portable (even if city / job changes)
  • Tax: own contribution 80CCD(1), 10% of salary (within ₹1.5 lakh, 80CCE) · employer's 80CCD(2), 10% over and above · extra ₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B)
  • Tier-I (retirement, tax benefit): ₹500 at opening and each time, at least ₹1000 a year or the account is frozen
  • Tier-II (savings, withdraw any time, no tax benefit): ₹1000 at opening, no yearly minimum
  • PRAN = 12 digits (from the CRA) · e-PRAN via the 17-digit acknowledgement · PAN for cash over ₹50,000
  • Charges: registration ₹200 · service ₹30 · withdrawal ₹125 · nominee change ₹20

🛡 Jan Suraksha schemes

PMSBY (accident insurance)
  • Age 18–70 · through one account only
  • Premium ₹20 a year, auto-debit before 1 June
  • Cover 1 June – 31 May
  • Death / total disability → ₹2 lakh
  • Public sector / general insurers
PMJJBY (life insurance)
  • Age 18–50
  • Premium ₹436 (Jun–Aug) · ₹342 (Sep–Nov) · ₹228 (Dec–Feb) · ₹114 (Mar–May)
  • Cover 1 June – 31 May · 30-day lien (except accidents)
  • LIC / life insurers
